The question you're actually asking

Could you just build this yourself?

Yes, and I'd rather say so on my own sales page. It's a folder, plain files, and rules in English. Nothing was held back today.

It costs months of evenings and about a hundred decisions where the wrong answer stays invisible for six months. Each has one right answer and four plausible wrong ones, and the wrong ones don't announce themselves. They quietly cost you the compounding. Plenty of people should do it anyway, and if that's you, good.

Straight answers.

Why is the room the same price as the one to one?

They cost me the same to run, and pricing the room lower would tax everyone who can't travel. Not in Melbourne? Take the build: same system, remote, more custom because yours is the only business in it.

How much of your time do I get?

About five hours across four sessions, plus two follow-ups and support between. The day is a full day plus two weeks after. What you're buying isn't the hours, it's which hundred decisions get made correctly inside them.

Do I get updates afterwards?

On the day and the build, every update for twelve months, nothing extra. The kit gets the version current at purchase; if a later one is offered I'll say so, but it isn't promised.

Can I start with the kit and upgrade?

Inside 30 days what you paid comes off in full, and nothing you set up is wasted, since the kit is the architecture the build starts from. After that it's a separate purchase.

What do I keep at the end?

Your files, on your machine, and your business in plain English you can change. What you build is yours outright. The kit itself you hold under a perpetual licence rather than owning its copyright. No platform, no fee to me. It runs on a paid Claude plan, so you rely on Anthropic the way you rely on your accounting software.

What's the catch?

Two or three weeks in, when it clicks, people stop sleeping properly. You'll run six things at once and cancel things you shouldn't. It wears off. Few people selling this mention that stretch. I just did.

What do I need before it works?

A Mac or a Windows PC, a paid Claude plan in your own name, and Git. Git is a small free tool you'll never open, a few minutes to install. On the build and the day we do it together.
