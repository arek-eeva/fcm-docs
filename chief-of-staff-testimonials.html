Isaac Viglione Founder, Bloom Films NAMED HIS MARTY

"This has changed how I run my business. I came in not really knowing what was possible, and Arek walked me through it patiently. It only took 30-60 minutes for my brain to start to open to all the possibilities.

The system itself does the stuff I used to avoid. It keeps track of where every lead and job is at, drafts emails and proposals in our actual voice, and remembers the details I'd normally lose between sessions. I named mine Marty, and it feels like having a second set of hands rather than another app to babysit.

The best part is it bends to how you already work instead of forcing you into someone else's setup."

Orville Founder, Greater Visuals SALES, OPS AND A POLICY HANDBOOK

"Arek has helped us put innovation at the forefront of our business. As a founder, I wanted to streamline both our sales and marketing processes as well as the operational side of the business, and Arek helped us build the foundations of an AI operating system that is already delivering real value.

We're now able to generate proposal documents directly from client calls, produce fast and accurate quotes using our existing pricing, create detailed operational briefs for contractors, and develop a comprehensive policy handbook that includes our Workplace Health and Safety policies and Code of Conduct.

I'm incredibly grateful to Arek for helping us get this off the ground. His guidance, practical approach, and ability to turn ideas into working systems have made the whole process far less overwhelming. I'm excited to continue building on what we've started."
