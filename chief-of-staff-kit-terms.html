The free plan doesn't include the part the kit uses. You pay Anthropic directly, we never see it, and it's the ongoing running cost of the whole system. It's separate from, and on top of, the price of the kit.

Git installed

Git is a small free background tool. You'll never open it or look at it. Claude needs it to work on files on your own computer, and your Chief of Staff uses it to keep a private history of your system, which is what lets any file be put back the way it was last Tuesday.

Installing it takes a few minutes and the instructions in the kit walk you through it step by step on both Mac and Windows. Worth knowing before you pay rather than after: it's the one part of setup nobody can do for you remotely, because it's a dialog someone has to click at your own keyboard, and it's the single most common place people get stuck. If that's not a few minutes you want to spend, the guided build and the Melbourne day both do the setup with you.

Python installed

Python is a second small free background tool, and like Git you'll never open it. Your Chief of Staff uses it constantly in the background: numbering your tasks, taking the snapshots that let a file be restored, and recording which business this copy is licensed to.

On a Mac it arrives with the Git install above, so there's nothing extra to do. On Windows it's a separate download that takes about two minutes, and the instructions in the kit walk you through it. It's worth naming here rather than leaving you to meet it after you've paid.
