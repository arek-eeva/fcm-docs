While you wait, ten minutes

Start the folder that decides how good yours turns out.

The files are the same for everyone. The material you feed it in the first session is what makes one useful and another one generic, and that's the one part nobody can do for you.

Make a folder on your desktop and throw things in it. No tidying, no format, no order:

  • Your price list, or the last three quotes you sent
  • Two or three emails you're proud of, and one you'd rather forget
  • Whatever you've written about what you do, however out of date
  • The names of the clients you'd take ten more of
