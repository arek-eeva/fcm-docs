How this document got made

This is the part worth watching, because it is the mechanism rather than the pitch.

It begins as the recording of one conversation. Nobody transcribes it and nobody opens a template. The system finds the right call among hundreds of files, reads the whole thing rather than a summary of it, checks what it hears against everything it already holds about the business, the price list and every earlier conversation, then writes the document and puts it through a set of checks before I have seen a word. The figures are verified against the price list rather than remembered. Where it cannot find something, it says so instead of inventing it.

What makes that possible is the months that came before it. It knows how I write, what I charge, what I have promised other people, and which claims I am not allowed to make. A blank chat window has none of that and never will, however good the model underneath it gets. That is the difference the whole thing turns on, and it is why session one is the heavy one.

Every identifying detail of the real conversation behind this page was stripped before it was published, and a check that reads the source and fails the page if anything survives runs as part of the build rather than sitting in a promise. That check is the reason this can be shown to you at all.
