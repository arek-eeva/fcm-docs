Your Homework

This is what to gather before you set your Chief of Staff up, at step 5 of the instructions. Rough is fine. You don't need to tidy anything up or turn it into a neat document, and your assistant works with whatever you bring.

What to collect

Past AI conversations where you did real work. Copy or export any chats where you drafted something useful, worked through a problem, or got output you actually used. These show us where AI already fits your work, and help us build on what's working rather than starting from scratch.

A few pieces of writing in your own voice. A good email you sent, a social post, a proposal, a short description of your work. It doesn't have to be polished. We use these to teach your assistant how you actually sound, so it drafts in your voice rather than a generic one.

Your prices and what's included. A price list, a rate card, a service page, whatever you have. Your assistant will need to know this to help with quotes, enquiries, and anything client-facing.

The handful of tasks you repeat every week. The things you do on autopilot: following up on enquiries, writing session notes (the business parts, not the clinical detail), sending reminders, posting content, checking your calendar. A rough list is enough. These are the first candidates for handing off.

The one job you'd most love to hand off. If there's a specific task that eats your time, drains your energy, or keeps slipping, name it. Make that the first thing you build around.

A note on confidentiality

Your Chief of Staff handles the business layer of your work: marketing, admin, scheduling, templates, content, correspondence. It doesn't handle client records, session notes, or any sensitive personal information about the people you work with.

When you're collecting writing samples or past work, please check each one before you bring it. If something contains a client's name, contact details, or any private information, either swap in a placeholder name or take that section out. You don't need to spend time on this; just be careful with anything that could identify someone.

Hand all of it over at step 5 and your Chief of Staff takes it from there.
