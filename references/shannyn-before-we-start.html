4. Bring your AI work across

You've done real work inside Claude and ChatGPT already, including the advisory board build. Let's carry it over rather than start cold. Paste the prompt below into each project, let it write a clean handover, and save what comes back into Ingest as a Markdown file. You're helping me hand over to a new AI assistant that will run the business side of my coaching and consulting practice. Using everything you know about me and my work from our chats and this project, write a clear, plain handover brief a new assistant could read to get up to speed.

Cover these, with a heading for each:
1. About me and my work: who I am, what I do, and one line on what makes my approach distinctive.
2. What I offer and what it costs: my programs, coaching and workshops, and pricing if you know it.
3. Who I serve: my clients and audience, and the problem I help with.
4. My voice: how I write and speak, the words and tone I use, with two short examples in my style.
5. My frameworks: the models, language and structures I use with clients.
6. What we've worked on together: the projects, drafts and tasks you've helped me with, and where each is up to.
7. My recurring tasks: the things I do regularly that an assistant could take off my plate.
8. My preferences: how I like to work, and what I do and don't want from an assistant.
9. Anything else important you know about my work.

Format the whole thing as Markdown so I can save it straight to a .md file. Use # for the title and ## for each section heading. Start with a title line naming which AI tool this brief came from, and today's date underneath it.

Give it to me as one complete document in a single block I can copy out in full. No preamble before it and no commentary after it.

Important: keep it to the business layer only. Do not include any client names, coaching notes, session detail, or anything private about the people I work with. If something can't be shared, leave it out and say so.

Then, for each one:

  1. Run it in each of your projects, in both Claude and ChatGPT. Each one remembers different things, so each will give you a different brief. Run it in an ordinary chat too, to catch anything sitting in general memory.
  2. Copy the answer, open TextEdit, and before you paste anything go to Format > Make Plain Text. This is the step that matters. TextEdit saves in its own format otherwise and the file arrives unreadable.
  3. Paste it in, then File > Save, and name it ending in .md. Something like claude-handover.md or chatgpt-handover.md. If it asks whether you really want to use that ending, say yes.
  4. Save it into the Ingest folder, alongside everything else. A separate file for each tool is exactly right.

If any of that gets fiddly, just paste the answers into an email to me and I'll turn them into files. It is not worth twenty minutes of your evening.
