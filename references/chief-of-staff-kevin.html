The two things you named

Build one

The marketing rhythm

"I'm typically awful at sales and outreach. It's not my specialism at all, and when I've got a full project pipeline, I just let that activity go to zero." It learns how you write and what you have actually delivered, then drafts and schedules on a cadence. The work that used to need your impetus turns up already made and needs your yes. It writes from your own record, which is the one reliable way of not sounding like a marketer.

Build two

The corralling

You stand up a project office from scratch every client, and your status updates are, your word, clunky: Friday calls with the analysts, then transcripts you work back through by hand. This reads those transcripts, pulls each project's state out of them and assembles your fortnightly review. Nobody else has to be talked into logging into anything.

And what you said counts as it working
"Prove the concept. Not necessarily just your tools, but also my ability to use them."

That is the bar I am building to. If you finish these six sessions and still need me to run it, I have failed at the part I care about.

The analyst portal, and why it is not a line on this quote

We spent a good part of the call on the bigger version: your own branded system, documents held securely, the client on one side and ten analysts updating their own project status on the other. It is a real thing and you should have it.

It is not in this price, and I am not going to quote you for it later either. By the time we are a few sessions in, that is a thing you will build yourself. Not a version of it, the actual thing. That is what these six sessions are for, and it is why I am not selling you a portal: I would rather sell you the ability to build the portal, and then the next one, without ringing me first.
