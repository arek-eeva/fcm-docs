Straight answers.

Do I need to be technical?

You won't write code. You should be comfortable with files, browser tabs, installing an application and following a checklist. If you already use ChatGPT or Claude most days and can explain how your business works, you're in the right range. Everyone in the room is at roughly that level, which is why the pace works.

What if I fall behind everyone else in the room?

Twelve seats is the cap for exactly this reason. The build is the same shape for everyone, so nobody is off on their own track, and ten is small enough that I can get to you the moment you're stuck. If something is still half-done when you leave, that's what the fortnight plan and the two weeks of support after the room are for.

What do I need to bring?

Your laptop, an AI subscription at the $100 a month tier, and your actual business: your client list, your prices, a few proposals or emails you're happy with, and whatever you've already built. You'll get a short setup checklist beforehand so none of that eats into the morning.

I've already built things with AI. Is any of that wasted?

No, and this is the part people get wrong. What you've built gets folded in rather than replaced. The reason your pieces don't talk to each other isn't that they're bad, it's that there's no architecture underneath them. That's what the day gives you, and your existing work is the first thing that goes into it.

What does it cost to run afterwards?

The AI subscription, paid to the provider at their standard rate. No retainer to me, no licence, no platform fee. The seat is the last invoice I send unless you ask me back.

Is the price and the date locked?

Yes to both. Wednesday 14 and Thursday 15 October 2026, in Melbourne, and $2,950 + GST. Twelve seats. Can I bring my co-founder or my 2IC?

Yes, and it works well. You each build your own, and they read the same shared files, so the two can never drift apart. It's a seat each, because each person walks out with their own working system rather than a shared one, and two seats together are $4,950 + GST rather than $5,900.

Yes. If you run a mastermind, a cohort or a peer group, a closed room of your own members is the version of this that works best, because the group already knows each other. That's a conversation rather than a form, so message me and we'll shape it.
